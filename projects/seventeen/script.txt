Last night, I found a note taped to my bedroom mirror. No handwriting. Just printed letters. It said: "Whatever is standing behind your reflection only moves while the lights are off."

I laughed it off... turned off the lamp... and looked into the dark glass.

At first, there was nothing. Just my own tired face.

Then, at seven seconds... the doorway behind me in the reflection went completely black. Even though my hallway light was on.

At twelve seconds... something tall leaned in over my left shoulder. Its chin rested right above my collarbone. And it smiled with too many teeth.

When I spun around to look behind me... the room was empty.

The scariest part? The note was gone. And my phone screen flickered on by itself... showing a live camera view of the back of my own head.

Your seventeen seconds are up. Don't look behind you.
