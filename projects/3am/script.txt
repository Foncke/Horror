Last night, I woke up at three A.M. to the sound of my own voice whispering to me from inside my closet.

At first, I thought I was just having sleep paralysis.

I lay frozen under my covers, staring at the crack in the closet door, waiting for my eyes to adjust to the dark.

Then, the voice spoke again. It was my exact pitch, my exact breathing... begging me not to make a sound.

"Whatever you do," it whispered from the shadows, "do not look at the foot of your bed."

My heart stopped. Slowly, without moving my head, I shifted my eyes downward.

Standing at the end of my mattress was a tall, pale figure wearing my exact pajamas.

It had my face, my hair... everything. Except its eyes were completely pitch black, and its smile stretched way too wide.

It leaned forward, resting its hands on my blankets, and spoke in a cold, hollow voice.

"Why are you listening to the thing in the closet?"

I didn't have time to scream... because the closet door slowly creaked open behind it.

Where will you go? To the bed or in the closet?
